Logline

A psychological thriller short about a man who gradually loses each of his five senses, and the emotional toll it takes on both him and his wife.

Synopsis

It begins with a domestic argument and a simple line: Felipe can no longer hear out of one ear. From there, he loses his senses one by one. Shut in with the clatter of his typewriter, he drifts away from the world and from his wife, who searches for a new way to reach him.
