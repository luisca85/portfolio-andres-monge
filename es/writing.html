    Outside the Wake

    Ensayo personal de creative nonfiction sobre mortalidad, duelo y la diferencia entre una muerte repentina y una muerte que llega después de una vida larga.

    “What a privilege it is to live a life so full that your body simply can’t keep up with your spirit.”

    I cut the conversation short with my cousin, whom I haven’t seen since I was about 10 years old. “I apologize, I wish I could stay longer but I have a wake to attend to.”

    One of my childhood friends was mourning his grandmother’s death… I ask myself, “Is there such a thing as a calm death?”

  4. 04

    Ensayo · Diciembre de 2025

    • Estratégico
    • Marca y narrativa

    When Nationalism Becomes a Trend

    Ensayo de creative nonfiction que analiza el auge de movimientos populistas y nacionalistas en Europa y su relación con una tendencia global hacia la soberanía, la expansión territorial y una política cada vez más construida como marca.

    “In a world where politics is designed like a brand, the struggle is not only over territory or policy. It is over narrative: who defines strength, who defines identity, and who draws the map of power.”

    As populist movements rise in Europe, a global pattern of assertive nationalism takes shape. Across Europe’s most powerful capitals, a political shift once considered beyond the boundary is now shaping the mainstream.

    In Britain, Nigel Farage’s Reform UK has converted years of rebellious campaigning into political relevance… In Germany, the AfD polls near a quarter of the electorate.
