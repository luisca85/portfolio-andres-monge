Logline

A dramatic short about a young Afro-Indigenous Latino navigating the prejudices of the society around him.

Synopsis

Nahuel puts on his headphones to tune out the boys at school and rides off through the city. At home, the tension follows him to the dinner table. Between both worlds, he looks for a place where he can simply belong.
